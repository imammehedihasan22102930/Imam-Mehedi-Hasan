01 / About

Bio

I'm Imam Mehedi Hasan, currently studying Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) at Jatiya Kabi Kazi Nazrul Islam University.

I build things that sense, move, and think — robots that follow people, machines that climb walls, and systems that quietly protect homes and power grids. My work sits at the intersection of embedded electronics, control systems, and practical automation, and I learn best by building a working prototype and pushing it until it breaks.

Outside of coursework, most of my time goes into robotics competitions and independent builds — the 16 projects below are the result.
